This file contains Information that is not relevant to the project but may help with general maintenance of the Mackie ONYX 1640.


Power Connector Pinouts:

From PSU To power-interposer-board and
From power-interposer-board To pod-IO-plane
1: +12V
2: GND
3: +5.25V
4: GND
5: +48V
6: +15V
7: +15V
8: GND
9: GND
10: GND
11: -15V
12: -15V

From pod-IO-plane to main-fader-board
1: +15V (connected to pin7 of PSU connector Above)
2: GND (connected to pin4&8 of PSU connector Above)
3: GND
4: -15V (connected to pin12 of PSU connector Above)
5: +15V (connected to pin6 of PSU connector Above)
6: GND (connected to pin9 of PSU connector Above)
7: -15V (connected to pin11 of PSU connector Above)



Power interposer board:
sitting Right next to the PSU
pin 1-5 go Right through (so does Nothing to +5;+12;+48 rails)
+15 and -15 rails are interupted by a MOSFET each (FR024N on -15; 25P03L on +15)
which are each controlled by the one Output of the 2068-dual-op-amp (pin1 of op-amp to -15; pin7 of op-amp to +15)

the mixer works without it connected, the In- and Output are pin-compatible, no data on long-term
